First 90 Days

How I'd show up from day one.

My approach before I build anything: understand what's actually there. Here's the framework.

Based on what I've learned from the role description and the context available to me, this is how I'd approach the first 90 days. The specifics will evolve as I learn more about Carta's current priorities, the team, and what's already in motion.

Days 1 to 30
Listen before building
  • Understand Vrushali's priorities, decision-making style, and what she needs from a CoS that she isn't getting today
  • Conduct a structured listening tour across Product, PMM, Sales, Finance, and Solutions Engineering: same questions, every conversation, to surface patterns not just anecdotes
  • Begin building the cross-functional relationships with PMM, Sales, and Finance that the CoS role requires, understanding their needs from Product before making any changes
  • Map the current operating rhythm: what forums and cadences exist, who owns them, what decisions they produce, and where decisions are happening in Slack threads instead
  • For each acquired team, understand the current state of their integration into Carta, what's working, what they valued about their previous culture, and where the gaps are
What good looks like

A map of learnings by team and stakeholder: what I heard, where the patterns are, and where the disconnects live between what leadership sees and what's happening on the ground.

Days 31 to 60
Synthesize and propose
  • Identify the most pressing operational problem to address first, what I've read suggests it may be the roadmap-to-marketing translation gap, and propose a discovery process before proposing a solution
  • Propose a recommended rhythm of business for the Product org based on what I actually found, not a template I brought in from outside
  • Based on what I learn, evaluate whether a hybrid planning framework could bridge the roadmap-to-marketing gap, and if so, develop a proposal with PMM and Sales input before socializing more broadly
  • Maintain a running dialogue with Vrushali throughout, sharing emerging patterns, pressure-testing hypotheses in real time, and delivering a formal written synthesis by day 45 that reflects 30 days of structured listening and ongoing calibration together
What good looks like

A written analysis with clear recommendations and active collaborative discussions with Vrushali on prioritization.

Days 61 to 90
Start building with alignment
  • Pilot the proposed operating cadence for Q3, running it as a test before making it the standard, with explicit checkpoints to evaluate what's working
  • For each acquired team, prioritize the integration gaps identified during the listening tour and develop a plan, one that leadership and the team are enrolled in together, not handed down
  • Establish a regular reporting cadence that gives Vrushali and the Product leadership team a clear, consistent picture of organizational health
What good looks like

Initial feedback from the approaches being tested, teams experiencing the new cadences, and early signals on whether the proposals are creating clarity or surfacing new questions worth solving.
