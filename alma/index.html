Why Alma

Expanding access to mental healthcare is one of the most critical challenges our society needs to address — and it's what drives me.

I've spent the last six years inside a behavioral health platform. I understand what it takes to earn a provider's trust, what gets in the way of therapists doing their best work, and why simplifying access to mental health care is both operationally hard and genuinely important.

Behind that is something more personal. My family came to this country as immigrants and had a difficult road navigating the healthcare system. Watching them figure it out is part of what drives me — and in the last six years, I found a calling in mental health specifically. Working in support of providers has made me feel close to the real impact this work can have.

Alma's model — making it financially rewarding for therapists to accept insurance — is exactly the kind of structural unlock that changes care at scale. I want to be part of making that work operationally, not just philosophically.
